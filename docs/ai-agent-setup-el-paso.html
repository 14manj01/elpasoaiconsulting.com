AI agent setup

An AI agent that works while you do.

ChatGPT dots, Claude Duet and Meta Muse are AI agents that keep working in the background: reading your inbox, drafting replies, chasing invoices. I help you choose one, connect it safely and put it to work.

From chatbot to coworker.

For the first few years, AI was a chat window. You asked a question, copied the answer and did the work yourself. In late 2026 that changed. OpenAI, Anthropic and Meta each released an agent: an AI with its own computer in the cloud that connects to your apps and keeps working after you close the window.

That is a real shift for a small business. An agent can read every new email, draft the replies, notice the invoice nobody paid and prepare you for tomorrow's meeting, all before you sit down.

It also raises the stakes. An agent with too much access, vague instructions and no approval rules can send the wrong email to the wrong customer. Setting one up well comes down to a handful of careful decisions. That is what I do with you.

A careful start.

I set agents up the way I would want one working in my own business.

  • Read first, act later. The agent begins by reading and drafting. It earns the right to send after you have seen its work for a week or two.
  • One job at a time. We start with a single routine, get it right, then add the next.
  • Money and passwords stay with you. Banking, payroll approval and credentials are not handed over.
  • You know how to stop it. Before we finish, you can pause it, disconnect an app and delete what it remembers.

Questions, answered.

What is an AI agent?

An AI agent is an AI model that carries out tasks instead of only answering questions. You connect it to apps such as your email, calendar and accounting software, give it a goal, and it does the steps, then checks with you before anything important is sent, paid or deleted.

Which is best: ChatGPT dots, Claude Duet or Meta Muse?

It depends on where your work already lives. Dots fit teams that use ChatGPT, Slack or Microsoft Teams. Duet suits people who run their day from email, calendar and documents. Muse fits businesses built on Facebook and Instagram. I compare them against your actual tasks before you commit.

Is it safe to give an AI agent access to my email?

It can be, if it is set up with care. I start agents with read-only access, turn on approval for anything that sends or spends, and keep them away from banking and passwords. All three products are built to ask before consequential actions, and none of them is perfect, so you review what they draft.

How much do AI agents cost?

As of late November 2026, Meta Muse is free up to a usage limit with paid plans at $20 and $100 a month. Claude Duet is included with Claude Pro, Max and Ultra plans during its research preview. ChatGPT dots come with ChatGPT Pro and Business plans, and OpenAI is testing them with some Plus subscribers. These terms change often, so I confirm current pricing with you first.

Do you set up other agents, such as Gemini or Copilot?

Yes. Google's Gemini Spark and Microsoft's Copilot agents work on the same principles: connected apps, background work and approvals. If your business runs on Google Workspace or Microsoft 365, one of those may be the better choice.

Will an agent replace my office staff?

No. An agent is good at preparation: sorting, drafting, gathering and reminding. A person still decides, approves and deals with customers. Used well, it gives your staff time back for that work.

Bioinformatics consulting

Bioinformatics help from a published scientist.

Hands-on training and guided work in sequence analysis, CRISPR diagnostic design and custom pipelines for labs, public-health programs and biotech teams, from a Ph.D. molecular biologist who writes his own software.

Book a free consultationAll training

The analysis is often the bottleneck.

Sequencing is cheap. Making sense of the output is where projects stall. A lab can have freezers of samples and gigabytes of reads, but only one overworked graduate student who knows how the scripts run.

I work alongside your lab. We design the assay or build the pipeline together, and your lab runs it. You own the code, the data and the accounts, and I do not run or manage the analysis for you afterward. I earned a Ph.D. in molecular biology at Baylor University, where I developed a CRISPR-Cas12 method for detecting mosquito-borne viruses and wrote the software to design its assays. I have worked at the bench and at the keyboard, so I understand both the biology and the code.

What we work on together.

CRISPR assay design

Cas12 and Cas13 guide RNA selection with off-target screening, paired with RPA primer sets, designed together with your team.

Sequence analysis

Alignment, comparison and annotation, worked through together until your team can produce figures and tables you can publish.

AI for the lab

Training

Questions, answered.

What bioinformatics services do you offer?

Training and guided work in sequence analysis, CRISPR-Cas12 and Cas13 guide RNA design, primer and isothermal assay design, DNA barcoding for species identification, custom Python analysis pipelines, and help applying AI tools to lab work. We do the work together and your lab runs it afterward.

What is PrimedSherlock?

PrimedSherlock is an open-source Python tool I wrote that designs highly specific CRISPR-Cas12 guide RNAs from RPA primer sets and screens them for off-target matches. It was published in BMC Bioinformatics in 2022.

Do you work with labs outside El Paso?

Yes. I work remotely with labs anywhere, and I can meet in person with groups in the El Paso and Las Cruces area.

Can you train my students or staff?

Yes. I taught university lab courses for four years and mentored undergraduate researchers in molecular and bioinformatics methods. I can teach Python for biology, reproducible analysis and specific techniques.

Can AI be used safely with unpublished research data?

Yes, with care. For unpublished or sensitive data I can show you how to set up AI models that run on your own hardware, and we install them together, so nothing is sent to an outside service.

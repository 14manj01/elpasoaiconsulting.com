Muse AI setup and training

Put Meta's Muse AI to work in your business.

Muse is Meta's personal AI agent. Set up well, it can draft your posts, sort your inbox and review your numbers. I connect it to your tools, set safe limits and show you how to use it.

Book a free consultationAll services

An assistant that does the task, not just the talking.

Most people know AI as a chat box: you ask, it answers. Muse works differently. You give it a goal, such as "draft next week's posts" or "tell me which expenses look off this month", and it does the work using the accounts you have connected. Then it comes back to you for approval.

For a small business already on Facebook and Instagram, that is a real opportunity. It is also easy to set up poorly: connected to too much, approving too little, or never used after the first week. I help you get the useful version.

What I do.

Account setup

Create and configure Muse for your business, separate from your personal accounts where that makes sense.

Connect your tools

Link your Facebook Page, Instagram, ad account, email, calendar and business software.

Approval settings

Decide what Muse may do on its own and what must wait for your yes.

Privacy review

Go through the data controls together so you know what is shared and what is not.

Starter tasks

Set up a few recurring jobs that fit your business, such as post drafts and weekly summaries.

Hands-on training

Practice with your own business until giving Muse a good instruction feels natural.

What El Paso businesses can use it for.

  • Social media. Draft posts and captions in English and Spanish, in your own voice, for you to approve.
  • Ads. Review which Facebook and Instagram ads are working and draft the next campaign.
  • Inbox and calendar. Flag the emails that need a reply and write the first draft.
  • Books. With QuickBooks connected, summarize the month and point out unusual expenses.
  • Planning. Turn last year's sales and campaigns into a plan for the next one.

Is Muse the right tool?

Not always. Muse fits best when your business already lives on Meta's apps. If your work is mostly documents and email, ChatGPT dots or Claude Duet may suit you better, and the AI agent setup page compares all three. For confidential records I would point you to private AI. If you sell locally, see also AI help for Facebook Marketplace.

Questions, answered.

What is Muse AI?

Muse is a personal AI agent from Meta, launched in September 2026. Unlike a chatbot that only answers questions, Muse carries out tasks for you using the apps and accounts you connect to it. It is available through the Muse app, the web and WhatsApp.

What is Muse for Small Business?

It is a set of business skills and connectors Meta added to Muse in late September 2026. Muse can connect to Facebook Pages, Instagram professional accounts and Meta ad accounts, along with tools such as QuickBooks, Shopify, Canva, Slack, Stripe and Notion.

How much does Muse cost?

As of November 2026, Meta offers Muse free up to a usage limit, with paid plans at $20 and $100 a month for heavier use. Meta sets those prices and may change them; my setup fee is separate and quoted in advance.

Can Muse post or spend money without asking me?

Meta says nothing publishes, sends or spends without your approval, and purchases need approval every time. How often it asks for other actions is a setting, and choosing that setting carefully is part of what I do.

Is Muse safe for my business data?

It depends on what you connect and how you set it. I walk you through the privacy settings, including the option that controls whether your conversations are used to improve Meta's models, so you can make an informed choice.

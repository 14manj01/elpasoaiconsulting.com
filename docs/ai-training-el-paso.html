AI training and workshops

AI training your team will actually use.

Hands-on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Copilot training for El Paso teams, built around the work your people already do. Taught by a former university instructor.

Book a free consultationAll services

Most AI training is a slideshow. This is practice.

Your company may already pay for ChatGPT or Copilot. The usual result is that two people use it every day and everyone else tried it once. The tool is not the problem. Nobody showed your team how it applies to their own job.

I taught university lab courses at Baylor for four years, and the lesson that stuck is simple: people learn by doing the work, not by watching someone else do it. So my workshops use your documents, your emails and your real tasks. People leave having already done something useful with AI.

What your team learns.

How to ask

Writing prompts that get a usable answer the first time, and how to fix one that misses.

Everyday uses

Email, proposals, summaries, meeting notes, spreadsheets, research and translation between English and Spanish.

When not to trust it

How AI makes things up, how to check its work, and which tasks still need a person.

Privacy and safe use

What is safe to paste into an AI tool, what is not, and which settings to change on day one.

Role-specific skills

Separate exercises for the front office, sales, marketing, operations and management.

A simple AI policy

A one-page set of rules for your business, so everyone knows what is allowed.

Formats.

On-site workshop

I come to your office in El Paso, Horizon City, Socorro, Sunland Park or Las Cruces. Bring laptops.

Live online session

The same hands-on format over video, for remote or multi-location teams.

One-on-one coaching

Private sessions for owners and managers, focused on your own work.

Follow-up clinic

A later session to answer the questions that only come up after people start using the tools.

Training that leads somewhere.

Once a team is comfortable with AI, they start spotting tasks it could take over completely. When that happens I can turn those ideas into working automation, or help you set up private AI for work that cannot leave the building. If you are still deciding where to begin, start with AI consulting.

Examples.

Illustrative setups that show what is possible. They are not client results.

Front office workshop

Staff bring real emails and learn to draft replies, summarize long threads and translate between English and Spanish, with a checklist of what never to paste into an AI tool.

Owner coaching

An owner learns to use AI on their own inbox, proposals and spreadsheets in private one-on-one sessions.

Sales team session

A sales team builds reusable prompts for follow-ups, call preparation and proposal first drafts.

Questions, answered.

Do you offer ChatGPT training in El Paso?

Yes. I run in-person workshops at your office anywhere in the El Paso area, and live online sessions for teams that are spread out.

Who is the training for?

Business owners, office staff, sales and marketing teams, and anyone who writes, researches or handles information at work. No technical background is needed.

Which AI tools do you teach?

ChatGPT, Claude, Google Gemini, Microsoft Copilot and Meta Muse. I teach the tool your business already has, or help you pick one first.

How long is a workshop?

That depends on your team and goals. A short introduction fits in a lunch hour; deeper, hands-on sessions take longer. We settle the format in the free consultation.

Do you offer one-on-one AI coaching?

Yes. Owners and executives often prefer private sessions built around their own inbox, documents and decisions.

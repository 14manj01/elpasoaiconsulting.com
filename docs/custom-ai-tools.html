Software that fits the way you work.

Most businesses should begin with ready-made AI products. They are cheap and quick to try. But ready-made products stop at the edge of your own systems. They cannot see your job tracker, your inventory database or the spreadsheet that really runs the company.

Custom AI tools close that gap. They give an AI model controlled access to your systems, so it can answer questions with your real data and carry out tasks on your behalf, within limits you set. With AI coding agents such as Codex, building one is now within reach of a small team. I teach you how.

Questions, answered.

What is an MCP server?

MCP stands for Model Context Protocol, an open standard that lets AI assistants such as Claude and ChatGPT use outside tools and data. An MCP server is the piece that gives an AI safe, defined access to one of your systems, for example your inventory, your schedule or a database.

What is an AI agent?

An AI agent is an AI model that can take actions, not just answer questions: look something up, fill in a form, update a record, send a draft for approval. Setting careful limits is a large part of building them well.

Which programming languages do you teach?

Mostly Python, along with Java and C#. We pick the language that fits your existing systems, and the AI coding agent writes most of it.

Who owns the code?

You do. It is written in your own accounts during the sessions, and I do not host or maintain it.

Can AI be connected to my existing software?

Usually, yes, if the software has an API, a database or a way to export data. In the free consultation I will tell you whether it is practical for the system you use.

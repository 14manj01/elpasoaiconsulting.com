Software that fits the way you work.

Most businesses should begin with ready-made AI products. They are cheap and quick to try. But ready-made products stop at the edge of your own systems. They cannot see your job tracker, your inventory database or the spreadsheet that really runs the company.

Custom AI tools close that gap. I write software that gives an AI model controlled access to your systems, so it can answer questions with your real data and carry out tasks on your behalf, within limits you set.

Questions, answered.

What is an MCP server?

MCP stands for Model Context Protocol, an open standard that lets AI assistants such as Claude and ChatGPT use outside tools and data. An MCP server is the piece that gives an AI safe, defined access to one of your systems, for example your inventory, your schedule or a database.

What is an AI agent?

An AI agent is an AI model that can take actions, not just answer questions: look something up, fill in a form, update a record, send a draft for approval. Agents need careful limits, which is a large part of building them well.

Which programming languages do you use?

Mostly Python, along with Java and C#. I pick the language that fits your existing systems.

Who owns the code?

That is set out in writing before work starts, so there are no surprises about ownership or licensing.

Can you connect AI to my existing software?

Usually, yes, if the software has an API, a database or a way to export data. In the free consultation I will tell you whether it is practical for the system you use.

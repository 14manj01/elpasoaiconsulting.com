Private and local AI

AI that keeps your data in your building.

A ChatGPT-style assistant that runs on your own computers. Your documents, client files and records never travel to someone else's servers. We set it up together in a guided session, and you own it and run it.

Book a free consultationAll training

Some information should not leave the office.

When you type into a cloud AI service, your text is sent to that company's servers. For plenty of work that is fine. For patient records, legal files, tax returns, personnel matters and trade secrets, many owners are not comfortable with it, and some are not allowed to do it.

Private AI solves this differently. The AI model itself is installed on a computer in your office. You ask it questions and give it documents exactly as you would with ChatGPT, but nothing is transmitted anywhere. Pull the network cable and it still works.

I use this approach myself. I built an open-source assistant that watches home security cameras using a vision model running on a home computer, and the images never leave that machine. I teach you the same approach, and we install it together in your office.

Honest about the trade-offs.

Private AI has an upfront hardware cost and needs someone to look after it. That someone is on your team. I help you choose the hardware, and we install the system together in a guided session, and I teach your staff to use and maintain it. You own the hardware and the accounts and run the system afterward. I do not manage it for you. Cloud AI is cheaper to start and often more powerful. Many businesses end up with both: cloud tools for ordinary work and a private system for the sensitive part. I will help you decide where that line belongs. If you need the private system connected to your other software, you can learn to build that connection in custom AI tools training.

Questions, answered.

What is private AI?

Private AI, also called local AI, is an AI model that runs on hardware you control instead of a provider's cloud. Questions and documents are processed on your machine and are not sent over the internet.

Is local AI as good as ChatGPT?

For everyday office work such as summarizing, drafting, searching your own documents and answering questions about them, current open models are very capable. The largest cloud models are still stronger at the hardest reasoning tasks, so I help you match the tool to the job and tell you where the limits are.

What hardware do I need?

It depends on the model and how many people will use it. Some setups run on a recent desktop or Mac you already own; shared office use usually needs a dedicated machine with a good graphics card. We size it together before you buy anything.

Does private AI make my business HIPAA compliant?

No single tool makes a business compliant, and I am not a lawyer. Keeping data on machines you control removes a third party from the picture, which removes one source of risk. Your compliance officer or attorney should review the final setup.

Does it need an internet connection?

No. Once installed, a local model can run with no internet connection at all.

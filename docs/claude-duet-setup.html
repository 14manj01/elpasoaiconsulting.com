Claude Duet setup

Start every morning with Claude Duet.

Claude Duet is Anthropic's proactive agent. It sends a morning brief, keeps watch on your connected apps and asks before it acts. We set it up together on your own account, and I show you how to hand work to it.

Book a free consultationAll training

An assistant that comes to you first.

Most AI tools wait for you to ask. Claude Duet starts the conversation. Each morning it looks across your email and calendar, picks out what most needs your attention, and can begin the first steps, such as drafting replies for you to review. Through the day it follows up on threads that have gone quiet and tells you when a task is finished or needs a decision.

You talk to it in one ongoing thread, typed or spoken. Hand it a small job, like clearing an inbox, or a large one, like a financial forecast or a slide deck, and it works out which apps to use. It runs on its own computer in the cloud, so it keeps working after you close the app.

Anthropic built Duet for professionals and small business owners with more on their plates than they can track. That describes most people who run a small business.

What we set up together.

Getting access

The right Claude plan, a waitlist entry under the matching email address, and setup on your phone when the invitation comes.

App connections

Gmail, Google Calendar, Drive, Slack, QuickBooks, HubSpot, Docusign, Calendly and Stripe, connected one at a time.

Your morning brief

Instructions on what you want to hear about, in what order and how often, so the brief is short and useful.

What Duet knows about you

Your business details, tone and standard documents saved on your Claude account so Duet starts with context.

Approvals

A walk-through of how approval requests look, so you know what you are agreeing to before you tap yes.

Usage planning

Duet's background work uses your plan's limits. I show you how to set it up so it is less likely to run out mid-week.

Decisions stay with you.

Duet reads, drafts and prepares on its own. Before it sends a message, makes a payment, signs a document or deletes anything, it shows you exactly what it is about to do and waits. When it gives you a number or a summary, it tells you which email, file or spreadsheet it came from.

It is still a preview, and I would rather say so up front. It can misread a thread. It checks your accounts at intervals instead of constantly. It does not make phone calls. I set expectations with you at the start, and we begin with drafts and briefs before anything is sent in your name. Duet runs on your own Claude account. You own it and run it after our sessions, and I do not manage it for you.

If your work is centered on ChatGPT or on Facebook and Instagram, ChatGPT dots or Meta Muse may fit better. The AI agent setup page compares all three.

Examples.

Illustrative setups that show what is possible. They are not client results.

A morning brief that fits

You tell Duet what matters: new customer inquiries, anything from your accountant, and today's appointments. Each morning it sends those first, with replies drafted for review.

Unpaid invoices in QuickBooks

“Check QuickBooks for invoices more than two weeks overdue and draft reminders for me to approve.” Duet lists them, drafts each email and waits for your yes.

Client meeting brief

“Pull together a brief on this customer before my 2 p.m. meeting.” Duet reads the email thread and their website and returns a one-page summary.

Calendar conflicts caught early

Duet notices that a site visit sits on the wrong day, or that a call overlaps a flight, and offers to move it before it becomes a problem.

Contract out for signature

“Prepare our standard service agreement for this customer and send it for signature.” Duet fills it in, shows you the document and sends it through Docusign after you approve.

Follow-up from the CRM

“Draft follow-up emails for every quote in HubSpot that has had no reply in ten days.” You get a batch of drafts in your own voice to review.

Questions, answered.

What is Claude Duet?

Claude Duet is a proactive AI agent from Anthropic, announced in October 2026 as a research preview. You message it the way you would message a person. It works in the apps you have connected, sends a brief each morning, keeps working on its own cloud computer when you step away, and asks before it acts for you.

How do I get access to Claude Duet?

As of late November 2026, Duet is a research preview with a waitlist. You need a Claude Pro, Max or Ultra plan and an invitation, and it is available in the United States and a few other countries. I show you how to join the waitlist with the right account, and we set it up together when your invitation arrives.

How much does Claude Duet cost?

During the research preview, Anthropic includes Duet with Pro, Max and Ultra plans at no extra cost. It shares your plan's usage limits, and its background work counts toward them, so heavy use can reach your limits sooner than chat alone.

What will Claude Duet not do without asking?

Anthropic designed Duet to show you what it is about to do and wait for approval before it sends a message, makes a payment, signs a document or deletes anything. Some actions are off-limits even with approval, such as trading securities or moving money between accounts.

What can Claude Duet not do yet?

During the preview it cannot make phone calls or send text messages to other people, you cannot watch or take over its browser, and it checks your accounts every few hours, so something urgent can arrive between checks. It can also misjudge what is urgent, so review its drafts.

Which apps does Claude Duet work with?

Duet uses the connectors on your Claude account, including Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Drive, Slack, QuickBooks, HubSpot, Docusign, Calendly and Stripe. For websites without a connector it uses its own browser.

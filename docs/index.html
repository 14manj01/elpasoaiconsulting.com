What works. What does not.

Owners who tried these tools say the same things afterward. These are the lessons that repeat.

Works

  • Payment reminders that go out on schedule, so you are not the bad guy
  • A reply to every new inquiry within minutes, even at night
  • AI answering only the calls you cannot take, with a short greeting
  • AI writes the draft and a person approves it
  • Moving information between apps so nobody retypes it
  • The boring back-office work nobody sees: costing, reports, manuals

Does not

  • AI answering every call. Regulars hang up and call someone else
  • AI-generated food photos and flyers. Customers notice
  • Agents left to run with nobody checking them
  • Automating a messy process. It only makes the mess faster
  • Reminders with no stop rule, chasing someone who already paid
  • A phone bot that will not hand over to a person

Questions, answered.

Do you do the work for us, or teach us?

I teach. In hands-on sessions we set the tools up together on your own accounts, using your real work. You own everything and run it afterward. I do not manage your systems, your website or your IT.

What can AI actually automate in a small business?

The repeated work around the edges of the real job: payment reminders, replies to new inquiries, appointment reminders, review requests, data entry from invoices and forms, inbox sorting and weekly reports. There is a full list of 65 things you can hand to AI.

Can AI really handle my invoices?

It can handle the routine part: reading supplier invoices into your books, flagging price changes, listing overdue customer invoices and drafting the reminders. A person still approves payments and anything sensitive. See AI for invoices.

Will an AI phone agent annoy my customers?

It will if it answers every call. The setups that work let people answer first and use AI only for the calls nobody can take, with a one-sentence greeting, a clear statement that it is an assistant, and an easy way to reach a person. See AI phone and chat answering.

Do you build websites or manage IT?

No. I teach you to build and update your own website with AI and to use AI as your first IT help. If you want someone to run those for you, an agency or a managed IT provider is the right choice.

How much does it cost?

The first consultation is free. After that the price depends on the training you need, and you get a written quote before any paid work begins.

Do I need to be technical?

No. I explain everything in plain English, and you leave each session with written steps your team can follow.

Is my business too small for AI?

Probably not. A one-person shop can use AI to answer the phone after hours, write estimates and follow up with customers. The smaller the team, the more an hour saved is worth.

Is it safe to put my business data into AI tools?

It depends on the tool and the settings. I show you what each service does with your data before you choose it. For sensitive work such as patient, client or financial records, you can learn to run private AI on your own computers.

Which AI tools do you teach?

ChatGPT, Claude, Google Gemini, Microsoft Copilot and open models that run locally through tools like Ollama, plus the always-on agents: ChatGPT dots, Claude Duet and Meta Muse. I am not tied to any one vendor.

Do you work with businesses outside El Paso?

Yes. I meet in person across El Paso County, Las Cruces and southern New Mexico, and I train remotely anywhere in the United States.

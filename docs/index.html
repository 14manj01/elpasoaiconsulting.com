Questions, answered.

What does an AI consultant do?

An AI consultant looks at how your business runs and finds the places where tools like ChatGPT, Claude or an AI phone agent can save time or money. I then help you choose the tools, set them up, connect them to the software you already use, and train your staff so the change sticks.

How much does AI consulting cost in El Paso?

The first consultation is free. After that the price depends on the project, and you get a written quote before any paid work begins.

Do I need to be technical to work with you?

No. I explain options in plain English, and everything I set up comes with instructions your team can follow.

Is my business too small for AI?

Probably not. A one-person shop can use AI to answer the phone after hours, write estimates and follow up with customers. The smaller the team, the more an hour saved is worth.

Is it safe to put my business data into AI tools?

It depends on the tool and the settings. I check what each service does with your data before recommending it, and for sensitive work such as patient, client or financial records I can set up private AI that runs on your own computers.

Which AI tools do you work with?

ChatGPT, Claude, Google Gemini, Microsoft Copilot, Meta Muse and open models that run locally through tools like Ollama. I am not tied to any one vendor, so the recommendation depends on your work, not on a sales quota.

Do you work with businesses outside El Paso?

Yes. I meet in person across El Paso County, Las Cruces and southern New Mexico, and I work remotely with clients anywhere in the United States.

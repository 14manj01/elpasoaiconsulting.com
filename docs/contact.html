What to expect.

  1. Choose a time

    Use the booking button to pick an open slot on my calendar. You get a confirmation by email.

  2. Tell me the problem

    Describe your business and what is taking too much time or costing too much money.

  3. Get straight answers

    I tell you whether AI can realistically help, what it would involve and what I would do first.

  4. Decide

    If you want my help after that, I send a written quote. If not, you keep the advice.

Helpful to have ready.

  • The one or two tasks you most wish would go away.
  • The software you use every day, such as your email, accounting and scheduling tools.
  • Any AI tools you have already tried, and what you thought of them.
  • Any rules you work under for privacy or record keeping.

None of it is required. If all you have is a hunch that AI could help, that is enough to start.

A powerful platform with a steep first week.

Foundry, and the Vantage platform built on it, can pull scattered data into one place and turn it into something a leader can act on. It is also large. New users face dozens of applications, unfamiliar words like "ontology" and "transform", and no obvious place to begin. Many accounts go unused after the first login.

I help people get past that point. We start from a question you need answered or a product you need to build, choose the right Foundry tools for it, and work through the build step by step until you can do it without me.

What I can help with.

Getting oriented

What each application is for, how projects and permissions are organized, and where to start.

Data pipelines

Cleaning and joining datasets in Pipeline Builder, or with Python and SQL transforms in Code Repositories.

Analysis

Answering questions in Contour and Quiver, and presenting results other people can follow.

Dashboards and apps

Building Workshop applications that people open every day because they save them time.

Ontology design

Modeling your data as real-world objects and links so everything built on top stays consistent.

AIP workflows

Using Palantir's AI Platform to add language-model steps to a workflow, with sensible checks around them.

How coaching works.

  1. Define the product

    We pin down who will use what you build and the decision it should support.

  2. Design it

    We sketch the data it needs, the steps to prepare it, and the screen the user will see.

  3. You build

    You do the building in your own environment. I explain each tool and technique with generic examples.

  4. Review

    We go over what worked, what to tidy up, and how to keep it maintained.

Your data stays where it belongs.

I do not ask for logins, screenshots of restricted data or exported files. Sessions cover concepts, design and technique. You carry out the work in your own account and follow your organization's rules for the platform at all times.

Questions, answered.

What is Palantir Vantage?

Vantage is the U.S. Army's enterprise data platform, built on Palantir's Foundry software. It brings data from many systems together so users can analyze it and build dashboards, applications and AI workflows.

What is the difference between Vantage and Foundry?

Foundry is Palantir's commercial data platform. Vantage is the Army's deployment of it, with Army data and Army rules. The core tools, including Pipeline Builder, Contour, Workshop and AIP, work the same way in both.

Do you need access to my data or my account?

No. I do not request or use access to government systems or data. Coaching is built on public documentation and generic practice examples, and you apply what you learn in your own environment under your organization's rules.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. Much of Foundry is point-and-click, including Pipeline Builder, Contour and Workshop. If you want to go further with Python or SQL transforms, I can teach that too.

Can you help a business that uses Palantir Foundry?

Yes. For companies with their own commercial Foundry environment, I can coach your team or work with them on pipeline, ontology, application and AIP design.

About

James Mann, Ph.D.

Scientist, teacher and software builder. I teach El Paso businesses to use AI in ways that hold up in real work.

Book a free consultationAll training

The short version.

I am an AI trainer and consultant based in El Paso, Texas. I hold a Ph.D. in molecular biology from Baylor University, I taught university lab courses for four years, and I write open-source AI software. El Paso AI Consulting gives local businesses practical, honest training in a technology that is usually sold with too much hype. I show you how, and we set the tools up together on your own accounts. You own them and run them afterward. I do not manage them for you.

How I work.

Science trains you to distrust a claim until you have tested it. I bring that habit to AI. Before I recommend a tool, I try it on the kind of work you actually do. When something does not hold up, I say so, even when it is the popular choice.

Teaching trained me to explain. Four years of leading lab sections taught me that people learn by doing, and that there is always a plain way to say a complicated thing. You will not get jargon from me, and you will not be left with a system nobody on your team understands, because your team sets it up with me.

Background.

Education

Ph.D. in Molecular Biology, Baylor University, 2022. B.S. in Biology, Montclair State University, 2017.

Teaching

Taught undergraduate biology courses and lab sections at Baylor University from 2018 to 2022, and gave guest lectures for Baylor and the Texas Department of State Health Services.

Research

Developed a gene-based test for detecting viruses (CRISPR-Cas12) and published the software behind it in BMC Bioinformatics.

Recognition

First place, Ph.D. oral paper, Entomological Society of America Southwestern Branch, 2022. First place, Ph.D. oral talk, Texas Mosquito Control Association, 2021.

Software I have built.

  • Creation Kit MCP server. A Model Context Protocol server (software that lets AI operate another program) with 80 tools that lets AI models generate large 3D game worlds, verified by 366 automated tests.
  • CamSentry MCP. Lets an AI model running on your own computer watch home security cameras and send alerts, without images leaving the machine. GitHub.
  • PrimedSherlock. A peer-reviewed Python tool for designing CRISPR diagnostic assays. Paper.
  • Accessibility Plus. An open-source game plugin that adds high-contrast text, colorblind-friendly shapes and text-to-speech.

I work in Python, Java and C#. More of my code is on GitHub.

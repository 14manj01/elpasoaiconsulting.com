AI chatbots and AI receptionist

Never miss a call or a customer.

An AI receptionist answers your phone and your website chat at any hour, in English and Spanish, books the appointment and hands the hard questions to you.

Book a free consultationAll services

The call you miss goes to your competitor.

When someone in El Paso needs an air conditioner fixed in July, they call the first company on the list. If nobody answers, they call the second. The same is true for a dental office at lunch, a law firm after five and a restaurant during the dinner rush.

An AI receptionist picks up every time. It answers the questions callers ask most, books the appointment straight onto your calendar, and sends you a text summary. A chatbot does the same job on your website, where many customers would rather type than call.

There are dozens of these products, and they differ a lot in voice quality, Spanish, price and how well they follow instructions. I help you choose one, set it up with your real information, and test it hard before your customers ever hear it.

What it can do for you.

Answer around the clock

Nights, weekends, holidays and the moments when every line is busy.

Speak English and Spanish

Callers are served in the language they choose, which matters in a bilingual city.

Book appointments

Connected to your calendar, so a caller hangs up with a confirmed time.

Answer common questions

Hours, prices, service area, insurance accepted, what to bring and how to prepare.

Hand off to a person

Emergencies and unusual requests are transferred or flagged right away.

Keep a record

Each conversation is summarized and sent to you, so no lead gets lost.

Who uses it in El Paso.

  • HVAC, plumbing, roofing and electrical companies that are on a job when the next customer calls.
  • Dental, medical and veterinary offices with a front desk that cannot keep up at peak hours.
  • Law firms that need intake calls answered and screened after hours.
  • Auto shops, salons and restaurants where the person who answers the phone is also doing the work.
  • Real estate agents and property managers fielding the same listing questions all day.

Set up the right way.

A badly configured phone bot is worse than voicemail. Before launch I write its instructions with you, load your real answers, place test calls in both languages, and try to break it. After launch I review real conversations with you and tune it.

Medical and legal offices have privacy rules to follow. I will tell you what a given service does with call recordings and transcripts so you can check it against your obligations. Want the receptionist to update your records too? That is workflow automation.

Questions, answered.

What is an AI receptionist?

An AI receptionist is software that answers your business phone in a natural voice. It greets callers, answers common questions, takes messages, books appointments on your calendar and transfers urgent calls to a person.

Can an AI receptionist speak Spanish?

Yes. Current AI phone and chat systems handle English and Spanish and can switch based on how the caller speaks. I test both languages with realistic calls before anything goes live.

How much does an AI receptionist cost?

There are two parts: the monthly fee you pay the phone or chat service, which varies by provider and call volume, and my fee for choosing, setting up and testing it. I give you both numbers before you commit.

Will callers know they are talking to AI?

They should. I set systems up to say so at the start of the call and to offer a human whenever the caller asks. Being upfront avoids frustration and keeps trust.

Can a chatbot answer questions about my specific business?

Yes. The chatbot is given your services, prices, hours, service area and policies, and is told to hand off to you instead of guessing when it does not know.

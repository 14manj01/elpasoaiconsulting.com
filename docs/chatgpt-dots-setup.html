ChatGPT dots setup

Put a ChatGPT dot to work for you.

A dot is OpenAI's always-on agent inside ChatGPT. It keeps working between conversations and messages you when it needs a decision. I set yours up to be useful and safe from the first day.

ChatGPT that keeps going after you log off.

Most people use ChatGPT like a search box. A dot is closer to an assistant with a desk of its own. It has its own computer and browser in the cloud, connects to the apps you approve, and carries work forward between conversations. When it finishes something or needs a decision, it messages you in ChatGPT, Slack or Microsoft Teams.

A dot can follow a saved schedule, react when something new arrives in a connected app, and split a large job into parts that run side by side. It can also look through your connected sources on its own to find ways to help. OpenAI limits that kind of self-started research to reading only.

The hard part is not turning it on. It is deciding what it can reach, what it may do alone, and which jobs are worth giving it. That is where I come in.

What I set up.

Plan and access check

Confirm your ChatGPT plan includes a dot and that the feature has reached your account.

Your dot

Create it, name it and connect it to the channels you use: ChatGPT, Slack or Teams.

App connections

Connect email, calendar, files and business tools with narrow permissions, such as reading mail without sending it.

Custom rules

Write the rules that say when the dot acts alone, when it asks first and when it hands the task to you.

Schedules and triggers

Save the recurring jobs with the right time zone and end date, and confirm what the dot recorded.

Privacy settings

Review memory and model-training settings so you know what the dot keeps and what OpenAI may use.

What stays in your hands.

OpenAI built checks into dots, and I set yours up to use them fully.

  • Sensitive actions come back to you. Changing a password or moving money is something you complete yourself.
  • Approvals are specific. Approving one message does not give the dot permission to keep contacting people for you.
  • You can watch and take over. You can open the dot's computer to see what it is doing and take control of it.
  • Stopping takes more than one switch. Pausing a dot stops its current task but not its scheduled runs. I show you where each of those is turned off.

Dots also have limits worth knowing before you rely on one. You cannot view or edit the individual notes a dot keeps; the only way to clear them is to delete the dot. Some websites block its cloud browser. And like any AI, it can misread an instruction, so the first weeks should be drafts and summaries, not sent messages.

Examples.

Illustrative setups that show what is possible. They are not client results.

Daily customer email digest

Slack request tracker

“When someone posts a service request in our requests channel, add it to the job list and tell me who it is assigned to.” The dot acts when a new message arrives.

Questions, answered.

What is a ChatGPT dot?

A dot is an always-on AI agent from OpenAI, launched in September 2026. It has its own cloud computer and browser, works through the apps you connect, keeps going between conversations, and messages you in ChatGPT, Slack or Microsoft Teams when it has results or needs a decision.

Which ChatGPT plans include dots?

As of late November 2026, a dot is included with ChatGPT Pro and Business plans, and OpenAI is testing dots with a small number of Plus subscribers. OpenAI decides access account by account and updates it often, so I check your plan and account before we plan anything.

Can a dot send email or spend money without asking?

OpenAI reviews any action that could affect your accounts or share information before it happens. The most sensitive actions, such as changing a password or transferring money, are handed back for you to do yourself. I add your own rules on top, so the dot asks before sending anything on your behalf.

Can a dot use my own computer?

Only if you connect it. By default a dot works on its own cloud computer. You can connect your computer from the ChatGPT desktop app so the dot can work with your files, and you can revoke that access at any time.

Does OpenAI train its models on what my dot sees?

According to OpenAI, on personal plans the “Improve the model for everyone” setting controls whether your dot's conversations and work may be used for training, and Business, Enterprise and Edu workspace data is not used by default. I walk through these settings with you.

How is a dot different from regular ChatGPT?

Regular ChatGPT answers when you ask. A dot keeps working when you are not there: it follows schedules, watches for new items in connected apps, and comes back to you with finished work or a question.

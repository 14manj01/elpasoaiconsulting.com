Questions, answered.

Are these real client results?

No. They are illustrative examples of what a business can do with AI after training, written to show what is practical today. They are not setups I build for you. Your results depend on your business, your tools and how the system is used.

Which example should I start with?

Start with the task that is frequent, repetitive and low-risk. Missed calls, inbox sorting and invoice reminders are common first projects because the payoff shows up within days.

Do I need to buy new software for these?

Often not. Many examples use tools you may already pay for, such as ChatGPT, Claude, Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace. In training we set them up properly and connect them to each other.

Can you help with something not on this page?

Yes. These are common patterns, not a menu. Tell me the problem in the free consultation and I will say whether AI is a good fit for it.

How do I know the AI will not make a costly mistake?

I teach you to keep a person in charge of anything that sends money or cannot be undone. AI prepares the work and someone on your team approves it.

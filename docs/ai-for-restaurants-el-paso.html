Questions, answered.

What can AI do for a restaurant?

The office work: entering supplier invoices, watching food costs, answering the phone when staff cannot, replying to catering inquiries, drafting social posts and keeping the website current. It does not cook, serve or replace your people.

Can AI track my food costs?

It can read your supplier invoices and tell you when the price of an item changes, which is where most surprises come from. It is only as complete as the invoices it sees, so the first step is getting them all into one place.

Will an AI phone agent confuse my customers?

Not if it is set up well. It says it is an automated assistant, answers common questions in English or Spanish, takes reservations and messages, and hands off to a person whenever the caller asks.

Does it work with my point-of-sale system?

It depends on the system. Many can export sales reports that AI can read and summarize. I check what yours offers before promising anything.

Is this affordable for a small family restaurant?

Several of these setups run on tools with low monthly plans, and some are free to start. My setup fee is quoted up front, and we begin with the one change that pays for itself fastest.

Do you work with restaurants outside El Paso?

Yes. I visit restaurants across El Paso County and Las Cruces, and most of the setup can be done remotely anywhere.

Local IT support

IT that just works.

Wi-Fi that reaches the back office. Email that works. Backups that exist. Someone local who shows up, fixes it and explains it in plain English.

Book a free consultationAll services

Small businesses do not have an IT department.

They have whoever is least busy. So the Wi-Fi password is on a sticky note, the router is the one the internet company left behind, nobody is sure when the last backup ran, and a new computer sits in its box for a month because setting it up is a chore.

None of this is hard to fix. It needs someone who knows what they are doing to spend a few focused hours on it and write down what they did. That is the service: plain, local IT support for offices, shops and restaurants.

What I handle.

Wi-Fi and networks

Coverage that reaches every room, separate networks for guests and staff, and a router set up properly.

Email and accounts

Business email on your own domain with Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace, and accounts for each person.

Backups

Automatic copies of the files you cannot afford to lose, with a test to prove they can be restored.

Security basics

Two-step sign-in, a password manager, updates and sensible settings on the devices you already own.

New computers and devices

Set up, updated, connected and ready to work, with your files moved over from the old one.

Printers and the rest

The printer that goes offline, the scanner nobody can find, the software that will not talk to the other software.

Why IT and AI belong together.

Every AI tool sits on top of ordinary IT. An AI agent that reads your inbox depends on your email accounts being set up correctly. An AI receptionist depends on your phone and internet. Private AI depends on a computer and a network you can trust.

When the same person handles both, AI projects stop stalling on small things like a forgotten admin password. It also means the security questions get asked before the AI is switched on, not after.

How it works.

  • A look around first. I check what you have: network, accounts, backups, devices. You get a short list of what matters, in order.
  • Fix the important things. We agree on what to do and what it costs before I start.
  • Write it down. You get a plain record of what was changed and where the passwords and settings live.
  • Stay in touch. When something new comes up, you know who to call.

Need more than a fix? See website design, invoice automation or AI agent setup.

Examples.

Restaurant Wi-Fi, untangled

Questions, answered.

Do you come on site?

Yes. I work on site across the El Paso area, including Horizon City, Socorro, Sunland Park and Las Cruces. Many quick fixes can also be done remotely.

What kind of IT problems do you handle?

The everyday ones a small business runs into: slow or patchy Wi-Fi, email and account setup, backups, security basics, new computers and devices, printers, and getting software to work together.

Is my business too small to worry about security?

No. Small businesses are common targets because they are easy ones. A few basics, such as two-step sign-in, a password manager, updates and tested backups, stop most of what goes wrong.

Can you set up Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace?

Yes. That includes business email on your own domain, shared calendars and files, and moving your existing mail over.

How do you charge for IT support?

You get a quote before any paid work starts. The first consultation is free.

Are you a 24-hour help desk?

No. I am one local person, not a call center. We agree up front on how to reach me and what happens if something breaks outside normal hours.

An assistant that comes to you first.

Most AI tools wait for you to ask. Claude Duet starts the conversation. Each morning it looks across your email and calendar, picks out what most needs your attention, and can begin the first steps, such as drafting replies for you to review. Through the day it follows up on threads that have gone quiet and tells you when a task is finished or needs a decision.

You talk to it in one ongoing thread, typed or spoken. Hand it a small job, like clearing an inbox, or a large one, like a financial forecast or a slide deck, and it works out which apps to use. It runs on its own computer in the cloud, so it keeps working after you close the app.

Anthropic built Duet for professionals and small business owners with more on their plates than they can track. That describes most people who run a small business.

What I set up.

Getting access

The right Claude plan, a waitlist entry under the matching email address, and setup on your phone when the invitation comes.

Connectors

Gmail, Google Calendar, Drive, Slack, QuickBooks, HubSpot, Docusign, Calendly and Stripe, connected one at a time.

Your morning brief

Instructions on what you want to hear about, in what order and how often, so the brief is short and useful.

Memory and skills

Your business details, tone and standard documents saved on your Claude account so Duet starts with context.

Approvals

A walk through how approval requests look, so you know what you are agreeing to before you tap yes.

Usage planning

Duet's background work uses your plan's limits. I help you set it up so it does not run out mid-week.

Decisions stay with you.

Duet reads, drafts and prepares on its own. Before it sends a message, makes a payment, signs a document or deletes anything, it shows you exactly what it is about to do and waits. When it gives you a number or a summary, it tells you which email, file or spreadsheet it came from.

It is still a preview, and it is honest to say so. It can misread a thread. It checks your accounts at intervals instead of constantly. It does not make phone calls. I set expectations with you at the start, and we begin with drafts and briefs before anything is sent in your name.

Some information should not leave the office.

When you type into a cloud AI service, your text is sent to that company's servers. For plenty of work that is fine. For patient records, legal files, tax returns, personnel matters and trade secrets, many owners are not comfortable with it, and some are not allowed to do it.

Private AI solves this differently. The AI model itself is installed on a computer in your office. You ask it questions and give it documents exactly as you would with ChatGPT, but nothing is transmitted anywhere. Pull the network cable and it still works.

This is work I do for myself. I built an open-source assistant that watches home security cameras using a vision model running on a home computer, and the images never leave that machine. I bring the same approach to your office.

Honest about the trade-offs.

Private AI has an upfront hardware cost and needs someone to look after it. Cloud AI is cheaper to start and often more powerful. Many businesses end up with both: cloud tools for ordinary work and a private system for the sensitive part. I will help you decide where that line belongs. If you need the private system connected to your other software, that is custom AI work.

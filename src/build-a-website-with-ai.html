Most small business sites lose the customer before the page loads.

A hungry person looking for dinner gives a restaurant's website a few seconds. If the menu is a slow PDF, the hours are buried and the page jumps around on a phone, they go back to the search results and pick someone else. The same is true for a plumber, a clinic or a shop.

Fixing that used to mean hiring an agency and waiting a week for every change. AI coding tools have changed the math. You describe what you want in plain English, the tool writes the code, and you check the result. An owner who has been shown how can keep a site current without calling anyone.

The site you are reading was built this way. It has no page builder and no tracking scripts, it follows your light or dark setting, and it runs on free hosting. The only yearly cost is the domain name.

What you learn.

Ask for what you want

How to describe a page, a layout or a change so the AI tool gets it right the first time.

Check what it wrote

How to preview the site on a phone, spot mistakes and send the tool back to fix them.

Put it live

Your domain name, free hosting and the steps that take a change from your computer to the public.

Keep it current

New menu items, new hours, a holiday notice, a whole new page. You make the change the day you need it.

Get found

Page titles, descriptions, a sitemap and your Google Business Profile, set up so search engines understand the site.

Stay in control

The domain, hosting and code are in your name, with a history of every change so you can undo one.

How the training works.

You build your real site during the sessions, with me beside you.

  1. Plan it

    We decide what the site needs to say and what a visitor should be able to do on the first screen.

  2. Build the first pages

    In a working session you direct the AI tool and I show you how to check and correct what it produces.

  3. Make a change alone

    You add or edit something while I watch, so the next time you do not need me.

  4. Run it yourself

    The site, the accounts and the know-how stay with you.

How Codex fits in.

Codex is OpenAI's agent for building software. You describe what you want in plain English and it writes and changes the code. That removes most of the slow, repetitive part of making a website.

It does not remove the need for judgment. Someone has to decide what the site should say, read what the tool produced and test it on a real phone. Those are the skills I teach. Other AI coding tools work the same way, and I will help you pick the one that suits you.

What I do not do.

I do not build sites for clients, host them or maintain them. If you would rather hand the whole job to someone else, a web agency is the right choice, and I will tell you so. If you want to own your site and change it yourself, this is the faster and cheaper road.

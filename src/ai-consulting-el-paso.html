What an AI consultant actually does.

Most business owners in El Paso have tried ChatGPT once or twice. Far fewer have changed how their business runs because of it. The gap is not intelligence or effort. It is knowing which of the hundreds of AI products fit your work, how to set them up safely, and how to get a busy team to use them.

That is the job. I sit down with you, learn how work moves through your business, and find the specific places where AI pays for itself. Then I tell you plainly what I would do first, what I would skip, and what it would cost.

Sometimes the answer is a twenty-dollar subscription and an afternoon of training. Sometimes it is an automation that saves a full day a week. Sometimes it is "not yet". You will get the honest version.

What you get.

A workflow review

A walk through your daily operations: phones, email, scheduling, quotes, invoices, records and reports.

A ranked shortlist

The opportunities worth acting on, ordered by payoff and by how hard each one is to put in place.

Tool recommendations

Which AI products suit your work, which plan to buy, and which popular tools you can ignore.

A risk check

What each tool does with your data, where AI makes mistakes, and which decisions should stay with a person.

A written plan

Clear next steps in plain English that you can carry out yourself, hand to your staff, or hire me to build.

Built for El Paso businesses.

  • Home services and contractors. HVAC, plumbing, roofing and electrical companies that lose jobs when a call goes unanswered.
  • Medical, dental and veterinary offices. Practices buried in scheduling, reminders and paperwork, where patient privacy comes first.
  • Law, accounting and insurance offices. Firms that handle confidential documents and need AI that keeps them confidential.
  • Logistics, freight and customs brokerage. Border trade produces mountains of forms, emails and status updates that are ideal for automation.
  • Restaurants, retail and real estate. Owners who need marketing, listings and customer replies handled without hiring another person.

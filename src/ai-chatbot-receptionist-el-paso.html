The call you miss goes to your competitor.

When someone in El Paso needs an air conditioner fixed in July, they call the first company on the list. If nobody answers, they call the second. The same is true for a dental office at lunch, a law firm after five and a restaurant during the dinner rush.

An AI receptionist picks up every time. It answers the questions callers ask most, books the appointment straight onto your calendar, and sends you a text summary. A chatbot does the same job on your website, where many customers would rather type than call.

There are dozens of these products, and they differ a lot in voice quality, Spanish, price and how well they follow instructions. I help you choose one, set it up with your real information, and test it hard before your customers ever hear it.

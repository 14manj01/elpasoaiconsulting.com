Set up the way that works.

The stakes are real. In one 2026 analysis of 16.7 million missed business calls, 69% got no callback within 48 hours (Quo, as compiled by Local Voice). But a badly configured phone bot is worse than voicemail, and owners who tried one say so loudly. The setups that hold up share a few rules, and I teach you to follow them.

  • People first. Your staff answer when they can. The AI takes only the calls that would have rung out.
  • A one-sentence greeting. Long greetings make callers hang up in the first seconds.
  • It says what it is. Callers are told they are speaking with an automated assistant.
  • A person is one step away. Anyone who asks for a human gets one, or a callback at a set time.
  • A narrow job. Take the details, book the time, confirm it. It does not guess at prices or invent openings.
  • Someone owns the follow-up. Every call lands with a named person and a deadline, not in a shared inbox.

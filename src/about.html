The short version.

I am an AI trainer and consultant based in El Paso, Texas. I hold a Ph.D. in molecular biology from Baylor University, I taught university lab courses for four years, and I write open-source AI software. El Paso AI Consulting gives local businesses practical, honest training in a technology that is usually sold with too much hype. I show you how, and we set the tools up together on your own accounts. You own them and run them afterward. I do not manage them for you.

How I work.

Science trains you to distrust a claim until you have tested it. I bring that habit to AI. Before I recommend a tool, I try it on the kind of work you actually do. When something does not hold up, I say so, even when it is the popular choice.

Teaching trained me to explain. Four years of leading lab sections taught me that people learn by doing, and that there is always a plain way to say a complicated thing. You will not get jargon from me, and you will not be left with a system nobody on your team understands, because your team sets it up with me.

The analysis is often the bottleneck.

Sequencing is cheap. Making sense of the output is where projects stall. A lab can have freezers of samples and gigabytes of reads, and one overworked graduate student who knows how the scripts run.

I work alongside your lab. We design the assay or build the pipeline together, and your lab runs it. You own the code, the data and the accounts, and I do not run or manage the analysis for you afterward. I earned a Ph.D. in molecular biology at Baylor University, where I developed a CRISPR-Cas12 method for detecting mosquito-borne viruses and wrote the software to design its assays. I have worked at the bench and at the keyboard, so I understand both the biology and the code.

What we work on together.

CRISPR assay design

Cas12 and Cas13 guide RNA selection with off-target screening, paired with RPA primer sets, designed together with your team.

Sequence analysis

Alignment, comparison and annotation, worked through together until your team can produce figures and tables you can publish.

DNA barcoding

Species identification from COI and other markers, including host identification from mixed samples.

Custom pipelines

Documented, repeatable Python workflows that we build together and your lab runs, turning raw data into results the same way every time.

AI for the lab

Language models for literature review, coding help and data cleanup, including private setups for unpublished data.

Training

Python for biologists and reproducible analysis, taught to students, technicians and investigators.

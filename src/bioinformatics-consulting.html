The analysis is often the bottleneck.

Sequencing is cheap. Making sense of the output is where projects stall. A lab can have freezers of samples and gigabytes of reads, and one overworked graduate student who knows how the scripts run.

I can take on that work or teach your team to do it. I earned a Ph.D. in molecular biology at Baylor University, where I developed a CRISPR-Cas12 method for detecting mosquito-borne viruses and wrote the software to design its assays. I have worked at the bench and at the keyboard, so I understand both the biology and the code.

Services.

Cas12 and Cas13 guide RNA selection with off-target screening, paired with RPA primer sets.

Sequence analysis

Alignment, comparison and annotation, with results delivered as figures and tables you can publish.

DNA barcoding

Species identification from COI and other markers, including host identification from mixed samples.

Custom pipelines

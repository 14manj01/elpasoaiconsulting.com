A day with it running.

Here is what an ordinary day can look like once the pieces are in place. It is an example, not a promise about any one restaurant.

  • 7:00 a.m. Your AI agent sends a short brief: two invoices entered overnight, one price change, one catering request waiting, and today's reservations.
  • 11:30 a.m. The lunch rush starts. Calls that would have rung out are answered, and two tables are booked for tonight.
  • 3:00 p.m. The delivery driver hands over an invoice. Someone photographs it and it is in the books before the truck leaves.
  • 4:30 p.m. You approve tonight's special post with one tap. It goes out in English and Spanish.
  • 10:00 p.m. You go home. The reminder for last week's unpaid catering invoice is already drafted for the morning.

What I will not teach.

  • No AI food photos or flyers. Customers read them as cutting corners. Your real food, photographed properly, wins.
  • No bot answering every call. Your host answers. AI covers only the calls that would have rung out.
  • No AI confirming big parties or changing prices alone. That is where a mistake gets expensive.
  • No numbers you cannot check. AI points out what to check. Your point-of-sale report confirms the figure.

Built for El Paso kitchens.

Back-office work is where restaurant owners keep using AI: recipe costing, weekly food cost, inventory, training manuals and reading the sales report. The National Restaurant Association's 2026 industry report found 26% of operators already use AI tools, most often for marketing and administrative work (Restaurant Dive).

Restaurants here serve customers in two languages, often with family running the front and the back. Everything we set up together works in English and Spanish, and it is simple enough that whoever is on shift can use it.

AI takes the paperwork and the missed calls, and gives you the evenings back. It does not replace your point-of-sale system, your accountant or the person who greets your regulars by name.

The tools behind this are AI agents such as Claude Duet, ChatGPT dots and Meta Muse. The accounts are yours and you run everything afterward. I do not manage it for you. If the Wi-Fi drops every Friday night, you can learn to troubleshoot it yourself with AI for IT help.

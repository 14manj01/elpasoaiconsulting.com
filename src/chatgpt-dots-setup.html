ChatGPT that keeps going after you log off.

Most people use ChatGPT like a search box. A dot is closer to an assistant with a desk of its own. It has its own computer and browser in the cloud, connects to the apps you approve, and carries work forward between conversations. When it finishes something or needs a decision, it messages you in ChatGPT, Slack or Microsoft Teams.

A dot can follow a saved schedule, react when something new arrives in a connected app, and split a large job into parts that run side by side. It can also look through your connected sources on its own to find ways to help. OpenAI limits that kind of self-started research to reading only.

The hard part is not turning it on. It is deciding what it can reach, what it may do alone, and which jobs are worth giving it. That is what I teach, and we work through it together in a session on your own account.

What stays in your hands.

OpenAI built checks into dots, and we set yours up together to use them fully. The dot lives on your own ChatGPT account. You own it and run it afterward, and I do not manage it for you.

  • Sensitive actions come back to you. Changing a password or moving money is something you complete yourself.
  • Approvals are specific. Approving one message does not let the dot keep contacting people for you. A recurring message needs its own approval.
  • You can watch and take over. You can open the dot's computer to see what it is doing and take control of it.
  • Stopping takes more than one switch. Pausing a dot stops its current task but not its scheduled runs. I show you where each of those is turned off.

Dots also have limits worth knowing before you rely on one. You cannot view or edit the individual notes a dot keeps; the only way to clear them is to delete the dot. Some websites block its cloud browser. And like any AI, it can misread an instruction, so the first weeks should be drafts and summaries, not sent messages.

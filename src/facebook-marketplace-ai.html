Marketplace rewards the seller who answers first.

Facebook Marketplace is one of the busiest places to buy and sell in El Paso, from trucks and furniture to tools, rentals and local services. It also eats time. Every item needs photos, a title and a description. Every listing brings the same handful of questions, at all hours, in two languages.

AI is very good at exactly this kind of work. It can turn your rough notes into a clean listing, translate it, suggest a fair price from comparable items, and draft a polite answer to "Is this still available?" for the fortieth time today. I set that up around how you already sell and show you how to use it from your phone.

What I set up.

Listing writer

Photos and a few details in, a complete title and description out, ready to review and post.

English and Spanish

Every listing and reply in both languages, so you reach buyers on both sides of the city.

Pricing research

A quick way to compare similar items and choose a price that sells without leaving money behind.

Reply drafts

Ready answers to the common questions, written in your voice, that you send with a tap.

Inventory tracker

A simple sheet of what is listed, what sold, for how much, and what needs relisting.

Better photos

Simple tools and habits for brighter, cleaner pictures that get more clicks.
